Our Story

Familiar flavours, packed with care

Sunny Bhuja House is a new food venture starting from Tajpur Fulwariya, Bihar, with a simple aim — to bring the Indian roasted snacks everyone already loves into cleaner, more convenient and professionally packaged formats.

It is run under Kushavaha Foods, the food business behind the brand. We start small and honest: local retail, neatly packed bhuja, WhatsApp ordering and home delivery in our neighbourhood.
