About Us

Our Story

A young snack brand from Bihar with an old-fashioned idea of quality.

Familiar flavours, packed with care

Sunny Bhuja House began in Tajpur Fulwariya, a small town in the Saran district of Bihar, with a simple observation: the snacks people already love — bhuja, roasted chana, moongfali, sev, chiwda — are usually bought loose, in plain bags, with no name to trust.

We felt the same familiar taste deserved better. Sunny Bhuja House is a new food venture started by Suraj Kumar Kushavaha, run under Kushavaha Foods, with the aim of bringing familiar Indian roasted snacks into cleaner, more convenient and professionally packaged formats.

We start small and honest — local retail, neatly packed bhuja, WhatsApp ordering and home delivery around Tajpur Fulwariya — and we plan to grow into wholesale and online ordering as we earn the trust of our first customers. No shortcuts, no tall claims. Just familiar flavours, packed with care.
